# Sample data

Small, public or synthetic datasets so you can build immediately. Nothing here is
proprietary or sensitive.

| File | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `green_river_rockport_wq.csv` | Grab-sample water-quality records (Green River at Rockport, KY), ~40 parameters, sparse — a realistic "messy real data" case. | Public U.S. water-quality monitoring records (USGS / Water Quality Portal style). Public domain. |
| `sample_sensor_stream.csv` | Synthetic high-frequency multi-sensor stream (dissolved oxygen, temp, pH, turbidity, conductivity, depth) with one low-oxygen sensor. | Synthetic — generated for this sandbox. Not real measurements. |

Want more? Pulling additional public datasets from USGS / EPA / the Water Quality
Portal is exactly what Track 2 is about. Keep anything you add public and cite its
source here.
